# Code of conduct

This is a small project about reading a public chain accurately. The bar for taking part
is the same as the bar for the code: say what you mean, show the evidence, and treat the
person on the other side as someone who wants the same thing you do.

## What is expected

- Argue about the work, not about the person doing it.
- Bring the receipt. A transaction hash, a failing fixture, a screenshot — a claim with
  evidence is worth more than a strong opinion without one.
- Take correction plainly, and give it plainly. Being wrong in public is the ordinary cost
  of working in the open.
- Assume the other person is doing their best with what they know. Ask before concluding.

## What is not

Harassment of any kind, including sexualised language or imagery, insults, sustained
disruption, publishing anyone's private information, or targeting someone for who they
are rather than what they wrote. Advice to buy, sell or hold anything is off-topic here
and unwelcome: this project reports what wallets did, and takes no position on it.

## Scope

Everything that carries the project: issues, pull requests, commits, code review, and any
place someone speaks for fomopulse.

## Reporting

Report privately through [GitHub's security advisories](../../security/advisories/new) for
this repository, which only the maintainers can read, or open an issue if the matter is
already public. Reports are handled by the maintainers, who will keep the reporter's
identity to themselves and say what came of it.

Consequences run from an edit request, through having a contribution declined, to being
blocked from the repository — proportionate to what happened and to whether it continues.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened to what a project this size can actually promise to do.
